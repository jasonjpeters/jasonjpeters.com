# About

I am a systems-minded web developer and graphic designer focused on building practical, durable websites, tools, and Linux-based systems.

My work moves between visual design, web design, development, and the systems that support them. I care about how things look, how they behave, how they are maintained, and whether they remain understandable after the first version is finished.

This site is a place to collect work that has often lived in too many separate places: graphic design projects, web work, technical notes, experiments, and writeups from systems I have built or worked through. Some projects come from a specific period of work; others are current notes from ongoing technical practice.

The common thread is practical making. I like clear interfaces, reliable systems, useful documentation, and work that can survive contact with real use.
